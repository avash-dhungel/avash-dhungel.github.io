A printable, branch-and-level field book template that turns raw surveying observations into clean, revisable reduced levels — so nobody re-books a level page because their columns drifted.

## Why

Surveying fieldwork at Pulchowk produces pages of backsights and foresights. Hand-drawn tables work, but a consistent template makes checking (ΣBS − ΣFS = first RL − last RL) genuinely quick, and keeps the record tidy enough to hand in.

## What's inside

- Standard level-book page with BS, IS, FS, HI, RL and remarks columns
- A separate chain-survey booking sheet for offsets
- A quick reference strip with the HI and Rise–Fall formulas

## Result

Print → clipboard → field → digitize at home. Pages scan cleanly into my digital notes.
