A structured journal for the basic electronics lab — each experiment documented as: aim, circuit sketch, expected values, measured values, and the deviation analysis. Built to survive the "explain why yours doesn't match theory" questions.

## Approach

Every lab session in a consistent format:

1. Aim & theory in one paragraph
2. Circuit diagram (drawn, then re-drawn in Falstad)
3. Expected values from calculations
4. Measured table
5. Deviation (%) + why (component tolerance, meter loading, wire resistance)

## What I learned doing it

Maintaining the journal changed how I close a lab: now I check component tolerance *before* the measurement, not after the report due date. Deviation analysis is where actual understanding lives — not in copying expected values.
