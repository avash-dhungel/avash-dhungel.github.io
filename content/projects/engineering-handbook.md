A living, searchable Markdown reference of the formulas, constants and definitions I use most — the one file I keep open while solving problems, instead of ten half-remembered PDFs.

## Motivation

Every exam week I re-scavenged my notes for the same handful of constants (E, G, K for steel; standard Maclaurin series; Mohr scales). This handbook is my attempt to end that scavenging once.

## Structure

- Mechanics & Strength of Materials
- Surveying formulae (positioning, levelling, traverse)
- Mathematics quick-references (series, trig identities)
- Physics constants
- Chemistry (electrochemical series, hardness conversion)

Short enough to skim, complete enough to trust. Constantly updated after every unit.

## Takeaway

A personal reference isn't a notepad copy of the textbook — it's the *filtered* version of what you actually forget. Making this made my study sessions faster than any online "formula PDF" ever did.
